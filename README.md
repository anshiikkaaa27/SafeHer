# SafeHer – AI-Powered Women Safety Application

## Overview
SafeHer is an AI-powered women safety mobile application designed to provide immediate assistance during emergencies through SOS alerts, live location tracking, emergency contact notifications, and intelligent voice-triggered safety mechanisms.

## Problem Statement
Women often face safety concerns while commuting, traveling alone, or encountering emergency situations. Existing solutions may not provide instant, intelligent, and accessible assistance when needed.

## Solution
SafeHer enables users to quickly seek help through a single-tap SOS system, AI-based voice activation, live GPS tracking, and automatic evidence collection.

## Key Features
- Emergency SOS Alerts
- Live Location Sharing
- Emergency Contact Notification
- Voice-Activated SOS
- Audio Recording
- Video Recording
- Nearby Safe Zones
- Fake Call Assistance
- AI Threat Detection
