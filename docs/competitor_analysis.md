## Existing Solutions and Competitive Advantage

Several women safety applications such as bSafe, My Safetipin, Life360, and 112 India provide features including SOS alerts, location sharing, and emergency contact notifications. While these applications offer essential safety functions, they primarily rely on manual user intervention during emergencies.

### Limitations of Existing Solutions

- Depend on manual SOS activation.
- Limited use of Artificial Intelligence for threat detection.
- Lack automatic audio and video evidence collection.
- Focus mainly on emergency response rather than prevention.
- Do not proactively identify distress situations through voice or behavioral analysis.

### Our Approach

SafeHer is designed as an AI-powered women safety platform that combines emergency response features with intelligent threat detection and automated safety mechanisms.

### Key Differentiators

#### AI-Based Voice Detection
Detects predefined distress keywords and triggers emergency actions without requiring manual interaction.

#### Automatic SOS Activation
Initiates emergency alerts and location sharing when a threat is detected.

#### Real-Time Location Tracking
Shares live GPS coordinates with trusted contacts during emergencies.

#### Automatic Evidence Collection
Records audio and video evidence during emergency situations.

#### Predictive Safety Features
Future versions will include AI-based unsafe area prediction using location trends and community-generated safety data.

### Conclusion

SafeHer extends traditional safety applications by integrating Artificial Intelligence, automated emergency response, and proactive safety measures. The goal is to provide faster assistance, reduce response time, and improve personal safety through intelligent technology.
